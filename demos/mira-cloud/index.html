Chapter one

The butterfly chase

Mira carried Cloud out into the garden, where the grass was warm and the flowers were nodding hello.

Then a little yellow butterfly floated by. Cloud's ears went up. His tail went up. And off he bounced, sure that today was the day he would catch one.

He didn't. But Mira laughed so hard she fell over, and that was even better.

Chapter two

A very bubbly bath

All that chasing made Cloud a little muddy. So Mira filled the tub with warm water, rubber ducks and a mountain of bubbles.

Cloud did not like the water. Cloud did like the bubbles. He swatted them, sneezed at them and wore one on his nose like a tiny hat.

By the end, the bathroom was wetter than Cloud.

And every day after that was an adventure.

Warm, fluffy and smelling of bubbles, Cloud curled up in Mira's arms. Best friends, from the very first hug.
