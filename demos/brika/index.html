Good questions

Before you snap it on.

Are the bricks really solid?

Yes. Each brick is moulded, polished and locked into the case, so nothing comes loose on your wrist.

Which size should I choose?

42 mm fits most wrists. Pick 38 mm for a slim wrist and 46 mm if you like a bold look.

Can I add or swap bracelet bricks?

Yes. Extra links come in the box, and colour packs let you mix your own bracelet.

How long does shipping take?

Every watch runs a 72-hour test first. After that it ships with tracking to your door.
