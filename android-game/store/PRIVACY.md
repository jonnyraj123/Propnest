# Privacy Policy for Duskhollow

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

## The short version

Duskhollow collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to do either.

## What the app stores

The only thing the game saves is your fastest escape time. It is written to the
app's private storage on your own device and is never transmitted anywhere. It is
deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-in, or profile.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No collection of your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers, or
  usage data.

The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it is not technically
capable of sending data off your device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback during scares. Nothing is recorded or transmitted. |

## Children

The game contains horror themes and jump scares and is not directed at children.
It collects no data from anyone, including children.
